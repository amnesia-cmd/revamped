FAQ

Questions, answered plainly

How fast can I get covered?

Most applicants receive a decision within 15 minutes. Digital policies are delivered the same day for approved applications.

Can I bundle multiple policies?

Yes. Complete and Legacy plans include bundle discounts up to 25% when you combine life, health, home, or auto coverage.

What if I need to file a claim?

File online or call our 24/7 line. Average first response is under 4 hours, with most claims resolved within 5 business days.

Is there a cancellation fee?

No. Cancel anytime with prorated refunds on unused premium. We believe trust shouldn't come with lock-in penalties.
